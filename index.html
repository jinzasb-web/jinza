About JINZA

Integrated Procurement & Wholesale Supply

JINZA TRADING SDN. BHD. is a Malaysia-based procurement, wholesale and international trading company providing multi-category sourcing and supply solutions for businesses.

Rather than focusing on a single product category, we work with an extensive supplier network to help customers source, consolidate and procure products across multiple industries.

Our supply capabilities cover industrial products, machinery spare parts, hardware, electrical products, construction materials, automotive and marine parts, tools, apparel, general merchandise and other commercial products.

From individual product requirements to mixed-category bulk procurement, we provide businesses with a more efficient way to manage purchasing through a single supply partner.
