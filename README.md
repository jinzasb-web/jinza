## 🏢 About JINZA

JINZA TRADING SDN. BHD. works with an extensive supplier network to help customers **source, consolidate and procure** products across multiple industries — rather than focusing on a single product category.

Our supply capabilities cover:

- **Industrial Products** — machinery parts, bearings, pumps, valves, electrical components, industrial hardware, tools and maintenance supplies
- **Machinery Spare Parts**
- **Hardware &amp; Tools**
- **Electrical Products**
- **Construction Materials** — construction materials, hardware, electrical supplies, coatings, waterproofing materials, adhesives and related products
- **Automotive &amp; Marine Parts** — automotive parts, commercial vehicle components, machinery parts, marine equipment and outboard engine spare parts
- **Apparel &amp; Textile** — workwear, uniforms, garments, textile products and other apparel for wholesale and commercial requirements
- **General Merchandise** — commercial products, accessories, consumables and other general merchandise sourced according to customer requirements

From individual product requirements to mixed-category bulk procurement, we provide businesses with a more efficient way to manage purchasing through a single supply partner.

## 🔎 Procurement Beyond Categories

Not every procurement requirement fits into a predefined product category. Our sourcing model handles a wide variety of products based on customer specifications, quantities, target prices and delivery requirements.

Customers can provide us with a:

**Product Name · Photo · Specification · Model · Part Number · Drawing · Sample · Product List**

…and our team will assist with sourcing and procurement. **Tell us what you need. We source it.**

## 🤝 More Than a Product Supplier

JINZA acts as a centralized procurement partner for customers who purchase products from multiple suppliers or across different product categories. We help simplify:

**Product Sourcing → Supplier Coordination → Price Consolidation → Order Management → Product Consolidation → Export &amp; Delivery**

## 🛠️ Local Development

1. **Clone the repository**

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/jinzasb-web/jinza.git
   cd jinza
   ```

2. **Open in browser** — double-click `index.html`, or use a local server (recommended):

   ```bash
   python -m http.server 8000
   ```

   Then visit `http://localhost:8000`
